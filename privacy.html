HomeBrief Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

HomeBrief is a personal project, built as a course project, that reads my own Google Calendar and speaks a short morning briefing on my Google Home speaker. It is used only by me, with my own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

What HomeBrief accesses

  • Google Calendar events on the account that signs in: their titles and start and end times, so the day can be planned and described.
  • HomeBrief can create focus block events on that calendar. Every event it creates is marked [claude]. It has no function to edit or delete events.

How the data is used

  • The day's event titles and times are sent to Claude (Anthropic), through Claude Code, to write the spoken briefing.
  • The briefing text is turned into speech with Google's text-to-speech service and played on my Google Home speaker on my home network.
  • While a focus block is running, its title and time may be shown as my Discord activity.

Storage and sharing

  • HomeBrief runs on my own computers. There is no HomeBrief server or database.
  • The Google sign-in token and the logs are stored only on those computers.
  • No calendar data is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in your Google Account under Third-party apps & services. Deleting the token file on the computer also stops all access.

Contact

Questions can be raised on the project's GitHub page: github.com/dxu13UW/HomeBrief.
